## Architecture and deslop assessment

The reservation triple is a useful representation, not duplicated model state. It distinguishes an installed SDK entry from the real model/paint owner and preserves the predecessor content without a portal or a copied frame cache. The source Listener consumes an explicit acceptance return rather than guessing from retained pointer state. No extra public knob, generic lifecycle framework, transfer model, timeline edit, new tuning or dependency is needed.

The source-onFrame post-callback guards are justified: sync may promote to the route or detach the source. The deferred exact-route retirement is justified by SDK/tree lock constraints; publishing stage state synchronously from disposal would be unsafe. Clearing the reservation before revocation/navigation callbacks is the correct reentrant ordering. Never-owning reserved disposal does not reset the predecessor's transfer. Initial reveal being deferred under an unrelated cover is a coherent bounded refinement after the observed late-autofocus failure: committed model work can proceed, while fresh content and autofocus are delayed until SDK currentness. Removing the broad FocusScope gate is preferable because it altered existing focus restoration.

No further concrete deletion or speculative abstraction is recommended from this diff. In particular, do not add a second mirrored `reserved` bool, a cached optical frame or an unconditional focus gate. `_routes` has the existing disposal-lifetime purpose; `_reservation` is one relation with different meaning. Some identity checks repeat because they protect distinct reentrant boundaries rather than merely narrating prior checks.
